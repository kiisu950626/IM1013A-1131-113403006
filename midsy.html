            17 Sustainable Development Goals        

       
           

                In 2015, the United Nations adopted the 2030 Agenda for Sustainable Development, outlining 17 core goals for global sustainable development.            

           

                These goals guide the decision-making, investment, and actions of governments, local governments, businesses, and civic groups over the next 15 years, working together to create a world where "every country achieves lasting, inclusive, and sustainable economic growth"—a world where natural resources are consumed and used sustainably.
