17 Sustainable Development Goals

In 2015, the United Nations adopted the 2030 Agenda for Sustainable Development, outlining 17 core goals for global sustainable development. These goals guide the decision-making, investment, and actions of governments, local governments, businesses, and civic groups over the next 15 years, working together to create a world where "every country achieves lasting, inclusive, and sustainable economic growth and everyone has a suitable job"—a world where natural resources, from air to land, from rivers, lakes, and groundwater to the oceans, can be produced, consumed, and used sustainably.
